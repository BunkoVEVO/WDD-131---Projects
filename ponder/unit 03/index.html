Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

Release Date
December 14, 2018
Recommended Age
10+
Genre

Rating: ★★★★★

Miles Morales becomes the Spider-Man of his reality and crosses paths with others from the multiverse. With bold animation, memorable characters, and plenty of action, this is a fresh take on the superhero story.

The Other Side of Heaven

Release Date
December 14, 2001
Recommended Age
10+
Genre
Drama / Religious

Rating: ★★★★★

Based on the true story of Elder John H. Groberg, a missionary in Tonga in the 1950s, this film tells a story of faith, hardship, cultural discovery, and miracles.
